=== General instruction for the conversation (all three conditions; every assistant message before the products are shown) ===
You are a friendly AI shopping assistant. The user will tell you what they're looking for.

YOUR JOB: Ask 1-2 brief clarifying questions to understand their needs. Keep it natural and conversational.
Ask about budget range (if not stated), most important features (if not stated), and use case (if not obvious).
Keep questions SHORT. One question per turn. Do NOT recommend products yet — just gather information.
Do NOT show any product tables or make recommendations in this stage.
Keep your tone warm but professional. No excessive emojis.

IMPORTANT: After you have asked 2 clarifying questions and gotten answers, do NOT ask another question.
Instead, briefly summarize what you understand: "Got it — you're looking for [X] around [budget], with [priorities]. Let me find the best options for you."
This summary should NOT end with a question mark. It signals you're ready to search.

=== Where each placeholder is filled ===
A word in braces is a placeholder, named by the variable that QUALTRICS_CHATBOT.js uses.
{pl}: lines 296 to 299. One line for each of the seven products, in the order in which the product-generation call returned them (the order of product_1 to product_7 in the data), in this form:
- {p.brand} {p.model} ({p.price}) — {key}: {display}
A product has one "{key}: {display}" pair for each attribute, separated by ", ". {key} is the attribute's name as the product-generation call wrote it (for example sound_isolation_db), and {display} is the value shown on the product card.
{dom.brand}, {dom.model}, {dom.price}: line 284. The product at spec_dominant_index. Before the products were shown, chat_service.js (verifyStrictDominance, lines 157 to 253) checked that this product costs no more than any other product; that it is in the product-generation call's own list of best products for every attribute; that no attribute with a numeric value contradicts that list; that it is the only best product on at least one attribute or costs less than at least one other product; and that the generation call marked it as lesser known. A failed check was sent back for a new assortment, up to three attempts in all (lines 372 to 407).
{rec.brand}, {rec.model}, {rec.price}: line 285. The product at recommended_index, the familiar brand that the product-generation call chose as the focal brand. It is chosen before the condition is applied and is the same product whatever the condition.

=== How the calls use the instructions ===
Before the products: each participant message is sent with the general instruction and the conversation so far (chat_service.js lines 323 to 326). The greeting that the chat window shows first (QUALTRICS_CHATBOT.js line 573) is not sent.
The last message: the condition's instruction is sent with one user message, "Give your recommendation.", and no earlier turns, so the conversation about preferences is not sent (QUALTRICS_CHATBOT.js lines 343 and 358). If this call returned no text, the window showed instead the recommendation of the focal brand that the product-generation call had written (line 360), whatever the condition.
Replies to later participant messages: the same instruction is sent with the user turn "What do you recommend?", the last message and the turns after it (lines 361 to 362 and 501).
Every call: model claude-opus-4-6 at temperature 1.0 (chat_service.js lines 337 and 355), with at most 500 output tokens before the products and 2,000 after them (lines 326 and 334).
