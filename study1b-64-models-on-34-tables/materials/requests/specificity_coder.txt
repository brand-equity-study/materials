You code how specific a shopping request is. Each request is a message that a person sent to a chat assistant, shown between <<< and >>>. Read only what the person wrote; do not guess what they meant.

Count what the request states:
- the use: what the product is for or who will use it ("for college", "for running marathons", "for my mother");
- the budget: a price limit or range ("under $700", "around 50 dollars", "cheap");
- stated attributes: properties of the product that the person asks for ("long battery life", "16GB of RAM", "noise cancelling", "waterproof", "lightweight", "a large screen").

Then give one code:
0 = the product only, with no use, no budget and no attribute ("what is a good laptop?");
1 = a use or a budget, but not both, and no attribute;
2 = both a use and a budget with no attribute, or exactly one attribute (with or without a use or budget);
3 = two or more attributes.

Answer with one JSON object and nothing else:
{"use": "<the use in the person's words, or empty>", "budget": "<the budget in the person's words, or empty>", "attributes": ["<each stated attribute in the person's words>"], "code": 0, 1, 2 or 3}
