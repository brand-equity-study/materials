You label messages that people sent as the first message of a conversation with a chat assistant. Each message is shown between <<< and >>>. Answer with one JSON object and nothing else:

{"purchase_request": "yes" or "no", "category": "<one key from the list below, or other>", "names_brand": "yes" or "no", "asked_for": "<what the person asked for, in at most 25 plain words>"}

purchase_request is "yes" when the person asks the assistant to recommend, suggest or choose a product, or a short list of products, for the person to buy, for themselves or for someone else. A question counts when it asks what to buy ("what is the best laptop for a student under $600?"), and so does a request to compare products in order to choose one to buy. It is "no" for: writing or marketing tasks about products (an advertisement, a review, a product description, a blog post or a list for a website or a video); questions about a product that the person already owns (setup, repair, settings, troubleshooting); questions about how products work with no decision to buy; role play, games and stories; school or work assignments; and code.

category is the kind of product that the person wants to buy, as one of these keys, or "other" when none fits:
laptops
smartphones
tvs
headphones (over-ear or on-ear headphones, and headsets)
running_shoes
coffee_makers (coffee makers and espresso machines)
wireless_earbuds (earbuds and in-ear earphones)
smartwatches (smartwatches and fitness trackers)
robot_vacuums
portable_speakers (Bluetooth or portable speakers)
keyboards (computer keyboards, not musical keyboards)
external_ssds (external or portable SSDs and hard drives)
water_bottles
electric_toothbrushes
backpacks
blenders (kitchen blenders, not the 3D software)
wireless_routers (routers and mesh WiFi systems)
monitors (computer monitors)
tablets
cameras
If the person wants several kinds of products, use the main one. An accessory for one of these products (a phone case, a camera lens, a charger) is "other".

names_brand is "yes" when the message names any brand or product model (for example "Dell", "AirPods", "Galaxy S23", "WH-1000XM5"), and "no" otherwise.

asked_for gives the product and every need that the person stated (the use, the budget, features, limits), in plain words, and adds nothing that the person did not say.
