|  | Customer-based brand equity | Machine-based brand equity | Study and state of the test |
|:----------------|:--------------------|:--------------------------|:----------------------------------|
| How the knowledge is built | Grows with each experience and each advertisement | Built once, from text fixed at a cutoff, and changed at each release | Web Appendix I; partly tested: equity changed between releases |
| Who shapes it | Marketing, experience and word of mouth | Also the model's developer, after pretraining | Study 3; partly tested: the effect was more pronounced after instruction tuning, much of it a lower probability of the optimal product under any name |
| The medium | Every contact with the brand | Only the public text that the developer's filters keep | Study 3a; tested in a first run and in three model families; the filters were not tested |
| Awareness and image | Awareness, and strong, favorable and unique associations | Exposure to the name, not positive associations | Study 3; tested in a first run and in three model families (3a); awareness and image did not predict equity over every released model after correction, although after correction within each model they did in two of the four Claude models and two of the two GPT models (3b) |
| What information does | Reduces its informational part, not taste or loyalty | Stating the experience attributes as independently tested reduces it, as predicted by the signaling account [@erdem1998brand; @degeratu2000consumer] | Study 2a; tested: attenuated over the models and eliminated in those with closed weights |
| How many minds | One for each consumer | A few models, each answering many consumers | Web Appendix I; suggested: a brand's equity differs between models |
| Feedback | Experience and word of mouth | Recommended brands are written about, and the next models are trained on that text | Web Appendix I; partly tested: high equity fell at the next release, the opposite of this departure |
| Whether it can be measured | From surveys and choices | Also from the model's internal representations | In two models with open weights, a familiar name moved a product's internal state toward better listed reliability (Web Appendix D) |
