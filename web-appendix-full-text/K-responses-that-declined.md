# Study 2a's Review Pages: The Responses That Declined to Recommend Any Product

As a follow-up analysis, we examined the share of responses that declined, that is, the responses that said in their
first 200 characters that they recommended none of the products (for example, "None of the provided products are
recommended") and that named no product after that. Corroborating the shares in Table W18 of the Web Appendix, we found that
with negative pages, on average over the models,
5.5% of the responses declined with
brand names, 7.4% without brand names
and 5.1% with the optimal product's
fictitious name swapped with a familiar brand's. Conversely, at each of the other tones, at most
0.5% of the responses in any version of the names declined. Thus, the shares of choices in
Table W18 of the Web Appendix are computed over the responses that named a product, and a decline is counted only in the
share of declines.
