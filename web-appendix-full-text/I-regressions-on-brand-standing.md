# Correlations and Regressions of Each Brand's Equity on Measures of the Brand's Standing

The correlations across brands are in Table W13 of the Web Appendix. In the 30 models and in the four Claude models, no
interval excludes zero. In the ten models with open weights, three intervals lie below zero (the frequency of the name
alone, page views and coded familiarity), and the upper end of the interval for page views is close to zero. After
Holm's correction over the analysis's 27 correlations, the
frequency of the name alone remains below zero (Holm-corrected *p*
= .013), and page views and coded familiarity do not. The ordering
of the brands is more similar between the 30 models and each of the two other sets of responses (the Claude
models' and the open models') than between those two sets: the Spearman
correlation is
0.52 between the 30 models and the Claude models,
0.36 between the 30 models and the open models and 0.10
between the Claude models and the open models. In the table below, we report six regressions of each brand's equity in
each of the 30 models on the brand measures, with a fixed effect for each category and standard errors clustered by
brand and by model, and no correction for the number of regressions. Being in a ranking of brand value had a coefficient of
