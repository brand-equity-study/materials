# Study 2a's Review Pages: The Evidence of Each Tone and the 15 Customer Reviews of Each Tone

We give the evidence of each tone verbatim below, for the optimal product of the laptops table in the messages of
Study 1c, and the 15 customer reviews that we wrote for each tone. Each block began "Retrieved from review sites:" and gave a review
site's rating and verdict, and then three customer reviews of the same tone. The 15 customer reviews of each tone formed
five sets of three, the product in each place of a table received a different set, and the sets moved by one place in
each order of the products, so no review appeared twice in a table and every version of the names received the same
sets. Tables without pages were shown as in Study 1a.
