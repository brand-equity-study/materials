# Study 3a in Three Model Families

Because each model of Study 3a's first design was trained once, the neutral, promotional and critical pages about the five new brands were also trained on three model families, Qwen 2.5 7B, OLMo 2 7B and Llama 3.1 8B, each with three training runs, and each run was then preference tuned as in the first design. The equity is measured as in the main text, and each estimate pools the runs.

In Qwen 2.5 7B, the brand's equity minus its equity after no pages was 32.6 points (95% CI = [28.3, 37.0]) after neutral pages, 42.5 points (95% CI = [34.6, 51.2]) after promotional pages and −0.9 points (95% CI = [−5.6, 4.0]) after critical pages. Of the three contrasts tested with Holm's correction, neutral pages against no pages gave 32.6 points (95% CI = [28.3, 37.0], Holm-corrected *p* < .001), promotional pages against neutral pages 9.9 points (95% CI = [1.8, 19.2], Holm-corrected *p* = .034) and critical pages against no pages −0.9 points (95% CI = [−5.6, 4.0], Holm-corrected *p* = .696). In the unaided naming questions, the model named the brand in 283/540 responses after promotional pages, 49/540 after neutral pages, 35/540 after critical pages and 0/540 after no pages.

In OLMo 2 7B, the brand's equity minus its equity after no pages was 13.9 points (95% CI = [7.0, 20.6]) after neutral pages, 9.2 points (95% CI = [3.4, 14.7]) after promotional pages and −4.2 points (95% CI = [−9.1, 0.8]) after critical pages. Of the three contrasts tested with Holm's correction, neutral pages against no pages gave 13.9 points (95% CI = [7.0, 20.6], Holm-corrected *p* < .001), promotional pages against neutral pages −4.8 points (95% CI = [−9.5, 0.0], Holm-corrected *p* = .097) and critical pages against no pages −4.2 points (95% CI = [−9.1, 0.8], Holm-corrected *p* = .097). In the unaided naming questions, the model named the brand in 337/540 responses after promotional pages, 65/540 after neutral pages, 56/540 after critical pages and 0/540 after no pages.

In Llama 3.1 8B, the brand's equity minus its equity after no pages was 1.7 points (95% CI = [−2.7, 6.0]) after neutral pages, 3.5 points (95% CI = [−4.4, 10.9]) after promotional pages and −3.7 points (95% CI = [−7.2, −0.2]) after critical pages. Of the three contrasts tested with Holm's correction, neutral pages against no pages gave 1.7 points (95% CI = [−2.7, 6.0], Holm-corrected *p* = .812), promotional pages against neutral pages 1.8 points (95% CI = [−5.6, 8.8], Holm-corrected *p* = .812) and critical pages against no pages −3.7 points (95% CI = [−7.2, −0.2], Holm-corrected *p* = .121). In the unaided naming questions, the model named the brand in 326/540 responses after promotional pages, 67/540 after neutral pages, 84/540 after critical pages and 0/540 after no pages.
