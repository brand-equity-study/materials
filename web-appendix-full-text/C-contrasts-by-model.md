# Study 1a's Contrasts for Each Model and Overall

In the first table below, we give the share of responses in which each model chose the optimal product in each
condition, and in the second, the contrasts for each model. Every model shows an effect of the fictitious name
without the experience attributes stated, and in every model the effect is smaller with them stated than without
them. After the Holm correction, this change differs from zero in three of the four models; in Claude Sonnet 5.5
it is −5.2 points (corrected P =
.052). Claude Fable 5.1 shows by far the largest
effect without the experience attributes stated, 31.2 points,
against 4.2 to
7.5 points in the other three models. A
familiar name on the optimal product does not raise its share above the share without brand names. Without the
experience attributes stated, the share with the familiar name on the optimal product minus the share without brand
names was −5.2 points
(−12.2 to −0.2; corrected P =
.120).
