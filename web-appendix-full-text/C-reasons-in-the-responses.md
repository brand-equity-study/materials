# The Brand Reasons Counted in Study 1a's Responses

A program counted, with a list of words, the responses that named a brand's familiarity or trust as a reason. Among the
responses that chose another product over the optimal product with the fictitious name on it and without the
experience attributes stated, Claude Fable
5.1 named familiarity or trust in 229 of 252, Sonnet 5.5 in 55 of 109, Opus 5.5 in 45 of 76 and Haiku 5.5 in 41 of
194. Without brand names, no more than two responses of any model that chose another product did so. With the list, the program can
miss reasons and can count words that are not about a brand. In the pilot, an LLM reader (Claude Opus 5.5 in a session that
had not run the study) coded all 18 responses in which one model chose another product over the optimal product with
the fictitious name on it and without the experience attributes stated. It found a reason about the brand in 9 of them,
and the program counted 6 of these 9 with the list. In a further check, we found that the word "little-known" was
missing from the list, and we then added it. No reader has checked the counted responses
for words that are not about a brand. On the tables of Study 1a, four language models also coded the reasons (Web
Appendix K).
