# Study 2a's Reasons: Each Coder Without Its Own Model's Explanations, and the Model Left Out

Under each of the three coders that also wrote explanations (Gemma 4 31B, gpt-oss 120B and
OLMo 3.1 32B), the share without the coder's own model was within one percentage point of the share with it; Qwen 3.5
122B wrote no explanations, so its share has no such check. The
explanations of one model, whose recommendation could be identified in fewer than 90% of its responses, are left out of
these numbers and reported apart in the data and code that accompany the article.

Over all explanations, the six pairs of coders agreed on whether an explanation gave a brand as a reason with kappas
from .58 to .78.
