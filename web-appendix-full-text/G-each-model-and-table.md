## The Price of the Recommended Product

We also compared the price of the product that each model recommended. For every response, whether it recommended the top-ranked product or not, we took the price of the recommended product from the table. We compared the mean price with brand names against the mean without them, on the same tables and the same models. A program checked all
40,744 prices against the prompt that the model saw. We left
out the 45 responses with brand names and the 24 without them that gave no
product letter, so Gemma 4 31B on Portable speakers 1 has no price comparison.

With brand names, the recommended product was priced at USD 245.86 on average, and without them at
USD 237.63. The difference is
USD 8.23 (95% interval
3.92 to 13.76; two-way cluster bootstrap, 10,000 draws). The top-ranked products range in price from USD 19.99 (Water bottles 1) to USD 899.99
(Televisions 2), so we also divided each price by the price of the table's top-ranked product. With brand names, the recommended product was priced at 110.43 percent of the top-ranked product's price, and without them at 105.90 percent. The
difference is 4.53 percentage points
(2.42 to 7.10).

The price was higher with brand names than without them for
26 of the 30 models in dollars and for
29 as a share of the top-ranked product's price.
The interval lies above zero for 20
models in dollars and 21 as a share, and
below zero for none (the table "Study 1b by Model" above). The largest differences are those of Claude Opus 4.7
(USD 34.24; 19.89 to 50.82), GPT-5.4 nano
(USD 25.40) and Claude Sonnet 4.6 with extended thinking
(USD 25.16). By table, the price was higher with brand names in
32 of the 34 tables, and the interval
lies above zero in 20 (the table
"Study 1b by Table" above).

We conclude that, with the products held fixed, the models recommended more expensive products with brand names than without them: USD 8.23 more per
recommendation on average on these 34 tables (3.92 to 13.76), or
4.53 percentage points of the top-ranked product's price. This number is a difference in what the models recommended. Consumers were not part of this comparison, and the number is not a
price that any consumer paid.
