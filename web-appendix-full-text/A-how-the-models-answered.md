# How the Models of Study 1a Answered

For Study 1a of Web Appendix A, this file contains how each group of models answered and how the
letter of the chosen product was taken from each response.

The estimates include 40 models from 13 developers:
5 Claude models, 4 GPT models and
31 models with open weights (each listed with its responses in Web Appendix
D). Four Claude models answered through Anthropic's API at the provider's default settings, with
adaptive thinking and no temperature set, on November 20, 2026, and a program took the product letter from the first
line of each of their responses. Claude Fable 5.2 answered through Anthropic's command-line tool and the GPT models
through OpenAI's coding tool, both of which place their own instructions around a request. The models with open
weights answered on a university's computers, seven of them on November 20 and the others on November 21 or after, and
where the first line of a response contained no letter, the letter was taken from the rest of the response by a second
rule.
